# Hotel Meridian: Night Desk Break-In

## The story

It is 3 a.m. at the Hotel Meridian. The night porter has gone home, the back office is locked, and the safe holds something you need. Luckily, the porter was careless. Somewhere in the login page are traces of who he is, what he used as a password, and the key card he left behind.

Get into the system, work out what the porter knew, and open the safe.

## Your goal

Capture the flag. It looks like this:

`FLAG{...}`

## How to play

1. Open `index.html` in your browser (or visit the link you were given).
2. Sign in with the right Staff ID and password.
3. Pass the verification step.
4. Enter the master key to open the safe.

## Rules

- Everything you need is on the page. No outside accounts, no downloads.
- Browser developer tools are allowed. In fact, you will need them.
- No attacking servers or other players. This is a puzzle, not a hacking contest.
- After 5 wrong attempts the form locks for 15 seconds, so guessing will not get you far.
- Do not share answers or the flag with other players.

## Stages at a glance

| Stage | What you do |
|-------|-------------|
| 1. Sign in | Find the Staff ID and decode the password |
| 2. Verify | Find the key card number and use it |
| 3. Safe | Combine what you proved to build the master key |
